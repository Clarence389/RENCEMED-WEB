Therapeutic Drug Monitoring Reference Table

High-yield TDM principles, sampling considerations, interpretation and safety monitoring.

TDM is more than measuring a concentration: clinical interpretation, correct sampling and the patient's clinical context are essential. WHO notes that concentration results complement rather than replace clinical judgement.

How to use these tables

  • Confirm the reporting laboratory's own reference interval before interpreting a result.
  • Document collection time, dose timing and relevant clinical context for TDM.
  • Interpret trends and the patient as a whole rather than reacting to a number in isolation.
  • Check renal/hepatic function and interacting medicines when relevant.
  • Escalate critical results according to the laboratory and clinical facility's notification pathway.
