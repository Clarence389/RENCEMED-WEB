Choosing a facility

Choosing a facility is an important part of safe, evidence-informed healthcare. Learn the basic principles, recognize situations that require professional assessment, and avoid using general educational information as a personalized prescription or diagnosis.

For patients & caregivers

Focus on what the topic means, what you should know before using a medicine or service, what questions to ask, and which warning signs should prompt professional assessment.

For students & healthcare professionals

Consider indication, contraindications, interactions, monitoring, documentation, adherence, communication and referral. Apply current local guidelines and patient-specific assessment rather than relying on a generic summary.
