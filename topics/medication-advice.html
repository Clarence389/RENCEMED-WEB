How medicines work

Medicines produce effects by interacting with biological targets, changing physiology or replacing a substance the body lacks. The effect depends on the medicine, dose, formulation, route, timing and the person receiving it.

Reading a prescription

A prescription may contain the medicine name, strength, dosage form, dose, route, frequency, duration and special instructions. If any part is unclear, confirm it with the prescriber or pharmacist before using the medicine.

Dosing schedules and missed doses

Regular timing can maintain an effective exposure for medicines that require consistent concentrations. A missed-dose plan depends on the specific medicine; do not automatically double the next dose.

Safe medicine storage

Self-medication and OTC medicines
