Clinical evidence framework

RenceMed prioritizes current guidelines, national regulatory information and reputable clinical reference services. WHO guidance is used for global public-health recommendations; NICE provides evidence-based guidance and medicines-optimisation resources; Kenya's Pharmacy and Poisons Board provides local pharmacovigilance reporting infrastructure; Medscape and UpToDate are included as secondary clinical-reference resources for further professional reading.
